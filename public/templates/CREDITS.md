# Template photo credits

Each catalogue-look template is made from a freely licensed photograph. The template is that photograph cut out,
straightened, cropped and turned into a light-and-shadow map (and, for some, tidied: a dress-form neck or museum tags
or a hanger removed, a printed texture softened). The same details are recorded in `index.json` under `credit`.

Templates made from a CC BY-SA photograph are themselves shared under that same licence; please keep this credit with
them.
