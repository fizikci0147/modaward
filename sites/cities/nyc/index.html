New York in layers

Every building footprint in the five boroughs, extruded to its measured roof height and coloured by the year it was built. A Dutch farmhouse in Flatbush, Gilded Age tenements, interwar Bronx apartment blocks and the supertalls of 57th Street all sit side by side. Hover any building for its date and height. Click it for its story and sources.

How it was made

  • 869,606 footprints, roof heights and construction years come from the city's building footprint layer.
  • Each footprint is joined to its tax lot in PLUTO for the address, floors, building class and whether it shares walls. Where the footprint has no year, the lot's year built is used.
  • Context layers place Overture Maps points of interest inside footprints, plus every individual landmark designated by the Landmarks Preservation Commission. Water and parks come from Overture; neighbourhoods are the city's 2020 Neighborhood Tabulation Areas.
  • Floors come from PLUTO when the lot has one building. Otherwise they're estimated as height ÷ 3.2 m.

Read with care

Most years before 1950 are estimates: the city records them rounded to 1920, 1925, 1930 and so on, so a lot of the outer boroughs shows up in the same few colours. Those buildings are drawn paler. The 25 landmarks with stories use their documented completion dates. 3,626 buildings have no recorded year and appear grey.
