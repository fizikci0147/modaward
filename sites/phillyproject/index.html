Philadelphia in layers

Every building footprint in the City of Philadelphia, extruded to its measured height and coloured by the year it was built. Penn's 1682 grid, the rowhouse city of the industrial era and the postwar Northeast all sit side by side. Hover any building for its date and height. Click it for its story and sources.

How it was made

  • 546,049 footprints and roof heights come from the city's building footprint layer.
  • Each footprint is joined to its property record by address. Where there's no address match, the record whose point falls inside the footprint is used. That links 98% of buildings to a year.
  • Context layers place Overture Maps points of interest inside footprints. Water, parks, neighbourhoods and the city line also come from Overture.
  • Storeys come from the assessment record. Where it has none, they're estimated as height ÷ 3.2 m.

Read with care

Three in four years in the assessment roll are estimates, and many are rounded to 1920 or 1925. Those buildings are drawn paler. The roll also records some large civic parcels poorly, so 24 landmarks such as City Hall, the Wanamaker Building and the Second Bank use their documented completion dates, and their story panels say so. 9,491 buildings have no recorded year and appear grey.
